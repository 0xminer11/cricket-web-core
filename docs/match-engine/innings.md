# Innings and results

Openers come from the first two batting-order positions. A wicket consumes the next unused position. Innings finish on overs, wicket cap or successful chase. Chasing target is previous innings runs + 1. A winning boundary is credited in full. No later delivery is accepted.

Defending margin = first score − chase score; chasing margin = configured wicket cap − wickets lost. In a tie, use the format setting. Initial formats play one Super Over pair (six legal balls, two wickets). The original chasing side bats first in that pair. A repeated tie remains a tie; no boundary-count rule or repeated unlimited rounds.

This is the Module 0 one-round simplified tie-break: regular rosters/lineups are reused, without later competition-specific nomination or carry-over eligibility rules. Future Test-like multi-innings formats are explicitly rejected instead of silently simulated as limited-over games.
