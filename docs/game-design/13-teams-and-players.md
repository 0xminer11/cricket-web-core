# 13 — Teams and Players

## Team definition
Static fields: teamId, name, shortName, region, careerTier, rating, battingStrength, bowlingStrength, aggression, logoAssetId, kitAssetId.

Initial fictional examples: River Hawks Academy, Metro Stallions, Coastal Blaze, Deccan Falcons, Capital Comets. No licensed real teams are required.

## Player roles
Roles are weighted interpretations, not hard locks:
- Opening Batter — defence/technique/timing/shot selection.
- Top-order Batter — timing/technique/placement.
- Middle-order Batter — balanced scoring and adaptability.
- Finisher — power/timing/placement/reflex with higher risk profile.
- Wicketkeeper-Batter — batting plus reflex/agility.
- Batting All-Rounder — batting-led mixed weighting.
- Bowling All-Rounder — bowling-led mixed weighting.
- Fast Bowler — pace/accuracy/seam/control/stamina.
- Swing Bowler — swing/accuracy/control/seam.
- Spin Bowler — spin/control/variation/accuracy.

Players may change primary role when their attribute profile and career events justify it. Secondary roles can be stored without changing underlying stats.
