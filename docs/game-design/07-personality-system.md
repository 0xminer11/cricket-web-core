# 07 — Personality System

## Stored personality traits
All use 1–100 and affect systems rather than cosmetics only.

- **Confidence** — small pressure-performance modifier and event option availability. High confidence may increase resilience but, with high Risk Appetite, can encourage ambitious choices.
- **Discipline** — training efficiency, fatigue-management choices, consistency of preparation.
- **Leadership** — captaincy event eligibility and teammate-focused event effects.
- **Professionalism** — coach/sponsor/contract trust and media-event consequences.
- **Risk Appetite** — preferred batting/bowling risk profile for AI/autoplay suggestions and event choices. Neither high nor low is universally best.
- **Team Mindset** — team-event outcomes and future chemistry/selection considerations.

## Not personality
Popularity is represented by Fans. Team chemistry becomes relationship/team state when needed. Batting aggression is modeled through Risk Appetite plus tactical context rather than duplicating a skill.

## Trade-offs
Event choices should shift traits in small amounts (typically ±1–4). Example: a defiant media response may +Confidence/+Fans but -Professionalism. A team-first response may +TeamMindset but sacrifice an individual opportunity. Clamp all traits to 1–100.
