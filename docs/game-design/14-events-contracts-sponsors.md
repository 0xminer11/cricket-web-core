# 14 — Events, Contracts and Sponsorships

## Event framework
Events are static definitions containing eventId, type, requirements, choices, effects, weight, cooldown, eligible tiers and repeatability. Runtime only stores event history/choice/result.

Initial seed includes 10 events across coach, media, selection, contract, sponsor, team, rivalry and milestone categories. Choice effects are small and bounded.

## Contracts
Contract fields: contractId, teamId, tier, salary Coins, match fee, performance bonus, duration in matches, expected role, minimum performance expectation and reputation requirement.

Contracts should normally be optional. Declining a contract may change timing/opportunities but should not permanently block the career.

## Sponsorship
Future-ready categories: Bat, Sportswear, Shoes, Energy Drink, Technology, Lifestyle. Eligibility derives from fans, tier, performance and Professionalism. Rewards may include Coins, cosmetic items, non-exclusive performance gear and bonus objectives.

Sponsor equipment follows the same equipment cap and cannot exceed competitive balance rules. Real brands are not required.
