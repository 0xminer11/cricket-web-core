# 10 — Economy

## Currencies
### Coins
Earned through matches, objectives, achievements and career rewards. Spent on performance equipment, deterministic upgrades and training.

### Gems
Premium currency. Earned mostly by purchase plus limited achievements/season rewards. Spent on cosmetics and optional convenience. Gems are never required to reach competitive cricket stats.

## Initial balance
**INITIAL BALANCE — SUBJECT TO PLAYTESTING**
- Starter: 2,500 Coins + 50 Gems.
- 2-over: 120 participation + 80 win + up to ~100 performance Coins.
- 5-over: 240 participation + 150 win + up to ~180 performance Coins.
- Basic training: 60–90 Coins; advanced training around 140+.
- Common gear: 300–700 Coins; Rare: 1,400–3,000; Legendary: 6,500–12,000.

Losses still pay meaningful participation/performance rewards. The economy must avoid forcing repetitive low-value matches to fund basic progression.
